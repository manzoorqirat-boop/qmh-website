Privacy

How we use the details you give on this website.

Draft. This is a plain-language starting point. Have it reviewed by a lawyer and edit it to match how your hospital works before you rely on it.

What we collect

When you book an appointment we collect your name, mobile number, age, gender, the reason for your visit and, if you give it, your email address.

Why we collect it

Only to schedule and confirm your appointment, to contact you about it (for example by WhatsApp, SMS or phone) and to prepare for your visit. We do not use it for advertising and we do not sell it.

Who can see it

Our reception and the doctor you book with. Your details are kept in the hospital's own system and are not shown on this website.

Payments

This website does not take any payment. You pay the consultation fee at the hospital.

Your choices

You can ask us to correct or delete the details you gave by calling or writing to the hospital. Contact details are on the home page.
